Before you write

  • Lost your records? If iCloud sync was on, turning it on again on the new device restores them. If you exported a JSON backup, import it under More.
  • A number looks wrong? Open the ⓘ button on the Calendar or Claim tab, or reopen the walkthrough from More. A day counts as absent only if you were outside the UK at midnight, which surprises people more than anything else in the app.
  • Is this tax advice? No. The app counts days. Whether you qualify depends on your employment, your vessel, your voyages and your residence, none of which the app can check. Confirm your position with HMRC or an accountant before you file.

Privacy policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

UK SED does not collect your data. There is no account, no sign-up, no analytics and no advertising. The app makes no network requests of its own, so nothing you enter is sent to me, and I have no way to read it.

This section sets out what the app stores, where it goes, and the two cases where something leaves your phone. Both of those happen only because you ask for them.

What the app stores

Everything you enter is held in a single file on your device:

  • Your days: a date, and whether you were inside or outside the UK
  • Your notes: any text you add to a day, such as a vessel, a port or a flight number
  • Your claim settings: adjustments you make on the Claim tab
  • App settings: appearance, whether reminders are on, whether iCloud sync is on, and whether you have seen the disclaimer and the walkthrough

The figures shown on the Home Screen widget are stored in a shared container as well, so the widget can read them. They stay on the device.

The app asks for nothing else. It does not use your location, your contacts, your photos, your calendar, your microphone or your camera, and it has no access to them.

Where it goes

Nowhere, by default. The file sits in the app's own storage on your phone. If you delete the app, iOS deletes the file with it.

There are exactly two ways data leaves the device, and you control both.

iCloud sync, which is off by default

If you turn on iCloud sync under More, the app copies your record into your own iCloud account so your devices stay in step. This uses Apple's iCloud key-value storage. The data goes to your iCloud, under your Apple Account. It does not come to me, and it does not go to any third party. I cannot see it. Apple's handling of it is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Turn the switch off and the app stops syncing.

Exports, which only happen when you tap Share

The app can produce two files: a PDF working sheet for your accountant, and a JSON backup of your record. Neither is created or sent automatically. When you export one, iOS hands you the standard share sheet and you choose where it goes: Mail, Messages, Files, AirDrop, or another app. Once it leaves, it is covered by whatever you sent it to, not by this policy.

The working sheet contains your day counts, your voyage log and any notes you have written, because that is the point of it. Treat it as you would any other tax document.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications, scheduled on the device by the app itself. They are off until you turn them on and grant permission, nothing about them is sent anywhere, and you can switch them off in the app or in iOS Settings.

Children

The app is rated 4+ and collects nothing from anyone, of any age.

Your rights

Because none of your data reaches me, there is nothing for me to hold, correct, export or delete on your behalf, and no data controller relationship arises over your record. You hold it yourself:

  • To see or export it: use the backup export under More
  • To delete it: delete the app, which removes the file. If you have used iCloud sync, turn the sync switch off first, or sign out of iCloud to stop it syncing further.

If you have used iCloud sync, the copy in your own iCloud account is yours to manage through your Apple Account settings.

Tracking

UK SED does not track you. It contains no third-party SDKs, no analytics, no advertising identifiers and no tracking domains. The privacy manifests inside the app bundle declare the same thing, and the App Store listing records "Data Not Collected". This page does not track you either: it loads nothing from anywhere else, so no other party sees that you visited it.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes what it stores or where it goes, this page will be updated before that version ships, and the date above will change with it.
